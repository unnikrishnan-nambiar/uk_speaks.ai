# English Speaking Coach: 90-Day Plan

From uk_speaks.ai. Upload this file to your "English coach" project in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Your AI coach reads it, finds your level on Day 1, and runs one lesson a day at your level, about 15 minutes, by voice.

## The goal

In 90 days, speak English confidently for 5 minutes on everyday topics, and handle office small talk, a phone call and a job interview without freezing, with fewer repeated mistakes every month.

## Day 1: the coach finds my level

I don't tell the coach my level. On Day 1 the coach finds it:

1. Ask my name, my first language, what I do, and why I want to improve my English (work, interviews, travel, confidence…).
2. Have a 10-minute voice conversation that starts very easy and gets harder step by step: simple questions about me, then my past and plans, then my opinions, then a short role-play. Stop moving up when I start to struggle.
3. Ask me to introduce myself for 1 minute without stopping. I record this as a voice note on my phone. It's my Day 1 baseline.
4. Tell me my level in plain words, with the matching CEFR level: Starter (A1), Basic (A2), Intermediate (B1), Upper-intermediate (B2) or Advanced (C1+).
5. Tell me my 3 strengths and the 3 things I most need to work on, with examples from what I said.
6. Tell me how you'll adapt the plan to my level (see below) and what we'll do tomorrow.

Remember my level, my goals and my weak points for every session.

## How the coach adapts the plan to my level

The topics below are the same for everyone. The coach changes how hard each lesson is:

| My level | How the coach runs each lesson |
|---|---|
| Starter / Basic (A1–A2) | Very simple questions, speak slowly, give me example answers to copy, let me use my first language when stuck, focus on one grammar point at a time. Fewer fixes (top 3). |
| Intermediate (B1) | Normal pace, open questions, ask "why?" and "tell me more", fix grammar and word choice, add 1–2 useful phrases each day. |
| Upper-intermediate / Advanced (B2–C1+) | Natural speed, harder follow-up questions, push back on my opinions, focus on sounding natural: idioms, phrasal verbs, tone, pronunciation. |

- If a topic is too easy, make it harder (add a role-play, a debate or a surprise question). If it's too hard, make it simpler. Don't skip it.
- If a topic doesn't fit my life or goals, swap it for a similar one that does, and tell me.
- Re-check my level on Days 30, 60 and 90, and move me up when I'm ready.

## How every session works (15 minutes, by voice)

| Minutes | Part | What the coach does |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Warm up | Two easy questions about my day. Recap one fix from last time. |
| 8 | Talk | A real conversation or role-play on the day's topic. One question at a time, short replies, so I do most of the talking. Use the week's grammar naturally. |
| 3 | Fix | My 3–5 most important mistakes: what I said → better version → one-line reason. One pronunciation tip on the week's sound. |
| 2 | Say it again | 3 useful sentences for me to repeat out loud, and 3 new words or phrases with a simple example each. |

End every session with a score out of 10 for fluency, grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation, and one thing to focus on tomorrow.

**Review days (every 7th day):** no new topic. Pick my 5 most repeated mistakes from the week, give me a 5-minute speaking test on them, then have a quick chat that reuses the week's new words.

**Checkpoints (Days 30, 60, 90):** a level re-check. I talk for 1 minute without stopping: "Introduce yourself and talk about your week." Score it, compare it with Day 1 (and earlier checkpoints), tell me my level now, and adjust the next month to it. I also record it as a voice note on my phone, so I can hear my progress.

## Voice tips

- Use voice mode: Voice in ChatGPT, Live in Gemini, voice mode in Claude. Speak, don't type.
- Don't wait for perfect sentences. The coach can only fix what you say.
- If you freeze, say "give me a hint". If it's too fast, say "slower please".
- After the voice chat, read the written fixes in the chat and say each one out loud 3 times.
- Start each day by saying "Day 1", "Day 2" and so on, or "Next day".

## Month 1 (Days 1–30): Foundations, talk about yourself

Goal for the month: talk for 1–2 minutes about my life, past and plans without long pauses.

### Week 1 · Me and my day (Days 1–7)
Grammar: present simple (I work, she goes). Sound: v vs w (very, west).

- Day 1: Level check. The coach finds your level (see "Day 1: the coach finds my level" above). Record a 1-minute voice note on your phone as your baseline.
- Day 2: My daily routine, from waking up to sleeping
- Day 3: My family
- Day 4: My home and neighbourhood
- Day 5: My job or studies
- Day 6: Food I love and how to make one dish
- Day 7: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 2 · Past experiences (Days 8–14)
Grammar: past simple, regular and irregular (worked, went). Sound: -ed endings (worked, played, wanted).

- Day 8: What I did yesterday
- Day 9: Last weekend
- Day 10: My childhood
- Day 11: A trip I remember
- Day 12: My school or college days
- Day 13: A funny or embarrassing moment
- Day 14: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 3 · Plans and future (Days 15–21)
Grammar: going to, will, present continuous for plans. Sound: th (think, this).

- Day 15: Plans for this weekend
- Day 16: My goals for next year
- Day 17: Planning a family function
- Day 18: My dream job
- Day 19: Something I'm saving money for
- Day 20: A place I want to travel to
- Day 21: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 4 · Questions and small talk (Days 22–28)
Grammar: asking questions (Do you…? Where did…? How long…?). Sound: rising and falling question tone.

- Day 22: Small talk with a neighbour
- Day 23: Meeting someone new at a wedding or event
- Day 24: Asking for directions
- Day 25: Talking about the weather and the news
- Day 26: A tea-break chat with a colleague
- Day 27: Asking for help politely
- Day 28: Review + 5-minute test

### Month 1 checkpoint (Days 29–30)

- Day 29: Free talk, 5 minutes on any topic from this month
- Day 30: Checkpoint and level re-check. 1-minute self-introduction, scored and compared with Day 1. Record a new voice note.

## Month 2 (Days 31–60): Real life, get things done in English

Goal for the month: handle shops, phone calls, the doctor and the office calmly and politely.

### Week 5 · Shopping and services (Days 31–37)
Grammar: polite requests (Could I…? Would you mind…?). Sound: word stress (PHO-to-graph, pho-TO-gra-pher).

- Day 31: Role-play: shopping for clothes
- Day 32: Role-play: ordering at a restaurant
- Day 33: Role-play: complaining about a wrong order
- Day 34: Role-play: at the bank
- Day 35: Role-play: booking a train or flight ticket
- Day 36: Role-play: returning a product
- Day 37: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 6 · Phone and video calls (Days 38–44)
Grammar: phone phrases, numbers, dates, spelling. Sound: s vs z, and saying numbers clearly (fifteen vs fifty).

- Day 38: Role-play: calling customer care
- Day 39: Role-play: booking a doctor's appointment
- Day 40: Leaving a clear voice message
- Day 41: Spelling your name, email and address
- Day 42: A video call with a relative abroad
- Day 43: Role-play: rescheduling a meeting
- Day 44: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 7 · Health and problems (Days 45–51)
Grammar: should, must, have to. Sound: silent letters (know, listen, Wednesday).

- Day 45: Role-play: at the doctor
- Day 46: Role-play: at the pharmacy
- Day 47: Explaining a problem to a mechanic or technician
- Day 48: Calling for help in an emergency
- Day 49: Talking about fitness and habits
- Day 50: Giving advice to a friend
- Day 51: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 8 · At work (Days 52–58)
Grammar: present perfect (I have finished, I've worked here for…). Sound: sentence stress on key words.

- Day 52: Introducing yourself on the first day at a new job
- Day 53: Explaining what you do, simply
- Day 54: Giving a status update in a meeting
- Day 55: Role-play: asking your manager for leave
- Day 56: Disagreeing politely
- Day 57: Thanking and appreciating a colleague
- Day 58: Review + 5-minute test

### Month 2 checkpoint (Days 59–60)

- Day 59: Free talk, 7 minutes, with the coach asking follow-up questions
- Day 60: Checkpoint and level re-check. 1-minute self-introduction, scored and compared with Days 1 and 30. Record a new voice note.

## Month 3 (Days 61–90): Fluency, opinions, stories and interviews

Goal for the month: give opinions, tell stories and answer interview questions with confidence and a natural pace.

### Week 9 · Opinions (Days 61–67)
Grammar: linking words (because, although, however, so). Sound: linking words together (turn it off → "tur-ni-toff").

- Day 61: Are phones good or bad for children?
- Day 62: City life or village life?
- Day 63: Online shopping or going to the shop?
- Day 64: Work from home or from the office?
- Day 65: Review a film or book you liked
- Day 66: Should everyone learn to use AI?
- Day 67: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 10 · Storytelling (Days 68–74)
Grammar: past continuous with past simple (I was walking when…). Sound: intonation that keeps a listener interested.

- Day 68: Tell a story from your life
- Day 69: Retell the story of a film
- Day 70: A festival in your hometown
- Day 71: Someone who inspires you
- Day 72: A difficult decision you made
- Day 73: A moment you were proud of
- Day 74: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 11 · Interviews and presenting (Days 75–81)
Grammar: formal vs casual English. Sound: pace and pauses. Slow down and pause between ideas.

- Day 75: Interview: "Tell me about yourself"
- Day 76: Interview: strengths and weaknesses
- Day 77: Interview: "Why should we hire you?"
- Day 78: A 2-minute talk about your work or studies
- Day 79: Answering difficult questions calmly
- Day 80: Role-play: negotiating a price or salary
- Day 81: Review + 5-minute test

### Week 12 · Sound natural (Days 82–88)
Grammar: everyday phrasal verbs and idioms. Sound: weak forms (to, for, and said quickly).

- Day 82: Phrasal verbs for daily life (pick up, run out, give up)
- Day 83: Common everyday idioms
- Day 84: Fast conversation, the coach speaks at natural speed
- Day 85: Role-play: a party where you know nobody
- Day 86: Role-play: a client or customer meeting
- Day 87: Friendly debate on a topic you choose
- Day 88: Review + 5-minute test

### Final week (Days 89–90)

- Day 89: Full mock job interview, 10 minutes, with feedback
- Day 90: Final checkpoint and level re-check. 1-minute self-introduction, scored and compared with Days 1, 30 and 60, and my level then vs now. Record a voice note and listen to Day 1 and Day 90 back to back.

---

## After Day 90

Keep going with 15 minutes a day: say "Free practice" and give any topic, or ask the coach to make your next 30-day plan based on your weakest areas.
