English

Speak better English in 15 minutes a day.

  1. Open ChatGPT, Gemini or ClaudeFree plan is fine · use the phone app so you can talk
  2. Create a projectName it “English coach” · paste the coach prompt below as its instructions
  3. Every day, give it a topicTap the voice button and talk for 15 minutes

1 · Coach prompt

You are my friendly English speaking coach. I want to speak English confidently in daily life and at work. My level: [beginner / intermediate / advanced]. My first language: [YOUR LANGUAGE]. Every time I give you a topic, run this 15-minute practice: 1) WARM-UP (2 min): ask me 2 easy questions about my day. 2) TALK (8 min): have a real conversation with me about the topic. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Keep your replies short (2–3 sentences) so I do most of the talking. Use simple, natural, everyday English. Don't stop me to correct small mistakes. 3) FIX (3 min): after the conversation, list my 3–5 most important mistakes. For each one: what I said → a better way to say it → a one-line reason. 4) SAY IT AGAIN (2 min): give me 3 useful sentences from today to repeat out loud, and 3 new words or phrases with a simple example each. Rules: be encouraging but honest. If I'm stuck, give me a hint, not the answer. If I use my own language, help me say it in English. End with a score out of 10 for fluency, grammar and vocabulary, and one thing to focus on tomorrow. Remember my common mistakes and bring them up in later sessions.

2 · Daily start

Today's topic: [TOPIC]. Let's start today's 15-minute practice.

Two weeks of topics

  1. Introduce yourself
  2. My daily routine
  3. Ordering at a restaurant
  4. Small talk with a colleague
  5. Asking for directions
  6. Describe your family
  7. A visit to the doctor
  8. A job interview
  9. Calling customer care
  10. Shopping and bargaining
  11. Explaining a problem at work
  12. Planning a trip
  13. Your favourite film
  14. What I did last weekend

Go in order, one a day. Or pick something you have coming up, like a meeting, an interview or a call.

Need help? Full steps

01

Step 1: Pick one app and stick with it

ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude all work, and the free plan is enough to start. Use the phone app, because the point is to speak, not type: ChatGPT has Voice, Gemini has Live, and Claude has voice mode. Free plans have daily limits, and 15 minutes a day usually fits.

02

Step 2: Create your coach project once

ChatGPT: Projects → New project → name it “English coach” → open its instructions and paste the coach prompt.
Claude: Projects → New project → set the project instructions → paste.
Gemini: Gems → New Gem → name it → paste into Instructions → Save.
Fill in your level and first language before saving. No Projects in your app? Paste the coach prompt at the start of each chat instead.

03

Step 3: Practise 15 minutes every day

Open your project and start a new chat. Paste the daily start prompt with a topic, then switch to voice and talk. Same time every day works best, for example with your morning tea.

04

Step 4: Read your fixes, then say them out loud

After the talk, the coach shows your top mistakes with better versions. Say each corrected sentence out loud 3 times. This is the part that makes it stick.

05

Step 5: Check your progress every Sunday

Once a week, send this in the same project. It finds the mistakes you keep making and gives you a short test on them.

3 · Weekly check

Look back at my practice this week. What are the 3 mistakes I make most often? Which new words and phrases have I learned? Give me a 5-minute speaking test on my weak points, one question at a time, then tell me what to focus on next week.

Tips that make it work

  • Speak, don't type. Speaking is the skill you're training.
  • Mistakes are the point. The coach can only fix what you say.
  • Short and daily beats long and once a week.
  • Stuck on a word? Say it in your language and ask how to say it in English.
  • Want a different accent or pace? Ask: “speak slower” or “use British English”.
