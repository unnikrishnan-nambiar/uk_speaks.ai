2 · Every new carousel

Carousel: [YOUR IDEA IN ONE LINE] Images: use the images I've attached.

Want images in your slides? Upload them in the same message, and Claude puts them on the slides. No images? Delete the “Images” line.

Other optional extras: the goal (saves, shares or comments), the number of slides, your own example, or a hook you already like.

Example: the hook makes the difference

Step by step

01

Step 1: Create your Carousels project (once)

In Claude, open Projects → New project and name it “Carousels”. Paste the project instructions from the top into the project's instructions, and fill in your handle, audience and colours. Add your logo to the project files.

Now every carousel starts as a new chat inside this project. Claude already knows your brand and the rules, and it remembers what you made before, so each post looks different.

02

Step 2: Start with one idea

A topic, a tool, an opinion or a screenshot. One line is enough. If you have your own example, a real result or a list in your order, give it to Claude. Your own take beats a generic list every time.

  • A tool you tested
  • A mistake people make
  • An opinion you hold
  • A screenshot of a result
03

Step 3: Pick the hook. Don't skip this.

The cover is one statement or one question: 3–7 words, true, readable at thumbnail size. No subtitle, no second idea. Ask for 5 options, each from a different formula:

FormulaExample
Self-test“Can you explain these 5 AI words?”
Mistake“Stop using ChatGPT for everything”
Confession“I was wrong about AI agents” (needs a real story)
How I“How I test an AI tool in 10 minutes”
Curiosity gap“The AI setting nobody turns on”
Challenged belief“Prompting isn't the skill anymore”
Tiny shift“One line that fixes AI answers”
Number + outcome“3 prompts that replace a meeting”
The good-hook test

Does it make the reader test themselves, disagree, or need the answer? If none, rewrite it. Skip flat “Top AI tools for…” hooks, hooks that insult the reader, and covers that need a subtitle to make sense.

04

Step 4: Pick a structure

Use a different one each time, so your posts don't all look the same:

  • Numbered playbook
  • Myth vs truth
  • Mistakes → fixes
  • Bad vs good prompt
  • “I used to… now…”
  • Quiz, then reveal
  • “I tested X”
  • Hot take + proof
  • Checklist to copy
  • Stop / start / keep

Whatever you pick, every slide has a job:

SlideJob
1The hook: one statement
2Stands alone and gives value right away. Instagram re-shows slide 2 to people who skipped slide 1.
3 to second-lastOne idea per slide
Near the endThe payoff: the reveal, the template, “the one most people miss”
Last (dark)One action that matches your goal
05

Step 5: Write short, then shorter

  • One idea per slide: 1–3 short sentences, or 3–5 bullets.
  • Screenshot test: every slide makes sense on its own and is worth sending to someone.
  • Add something to save: a prompt to copy, a checklist or a template.
  • Minimal wins. If a line doesn't add something new, cut it.
  • Never invent stats or results. Use [PLACEHOLDER] and fill in your real numbers.
  • One call to action only. For saves: “Save this for your next…”. For shares: “Send this to someone who…”. For comments: one easy question.
06

Step 6: Same brand, fresh look

Keep your brand the same every time: background, colours, font, size 1080×1440, handle, and a dark last slide. Inside that, give each carousel its own theme so your grid doesn't repeat:

  • Giant numbers
  • Chat bubbles
  • Receipt
  • Sticky notes
  • Terminal window
  • Magazine cover
  • Before / after
  • Scoreboard

For images, upload your own. Don't let Claude draw brand logos or characters.

07

Step 7: Check before you post

Look at every slide yourself: clipped text, empty gaps, too many colours, unreadable as a small thumbnail. Then send this:

Check prompt

Run a virality check on this carousel: hook ✓/✗, slide 2 works on its own ✓/✗, payoff comes late ✓/✗, only one call to action ✓/✗, every slide worth sharing ✓/✗. Fix anything marked ✗ first, then show me the slides again.

08

Step 8: Post it

  • Slides: download them as separate images, in order.
  • Caption: 2–4 short lines that don't repeat the slides, ending with an easy question.
  • Hashtags: 5–10. Check each one in Instagram search: aim for 100K–500K posts and skip anything over 1M.
  • Audio: match the mood (calm lo-fi for tips, uplifting for stories) and add it with Add music. It doesn't need to be trending.
  • Time: check Insights → Most active times. If unsure, post on a weekday morning or at lunch.
Tip

Keep the next one different. Always start new carousels inside your Carousels project. If one starts to look like the last, say: “Use a different hook formula, structure and theme from my last carousel.”
