EXCEL

Boring Excel → a clean dashboard. Free.

  1. Open Gemini or ChatGPTFree plan works. Gemini: turn on Canvas
  2. Upload your Excel fileTap + → pick your .xlsx or .csv
  3. Paste the prompt below and sendTap Preview to see your dashboard

The prompt

Turn the Excel file I uploaded into a clean, modern, interactive dashboard as a single HTML page. Rules: - Use ONLY the data in my file. Keep every row and number exactly as it is. Don't invent or round anything. - First, tell me in 3 lines what the sheet contains and which columns you'll use. - Top: 3–4 summary cards with the most important totals. - Middle: 2–3 charts that fit the data (e.g. progress, status, timeline, totals by category). - Bottom: the full table with search and sort. - Mobile-friendly, light background, one accent colour, clear labels. - Show it as a live preview I can open and share.

Project plan? Add this

It's a project plan. Add a timeline (Gantt-style) bar for each task, a progress ring for % done, and colour the status: Done = green, In progress = yellow, Not started = grey. Highlight anything overdue in red.

Sales or expenses? Add this

It's sales / expense data. Show totals by month as a bar chart, the top 5 items or customers, and a filter by month and category.

Example · same data, new look

Option A · Gemini (best for sharing)

01

Step 1: Open Gemini and turn on Canvas

Go to gemini.google.com or open the Gemini app and sign in with your Google account. In the message box, tap Tools (or +) and choose Canvas.

02

Step 2: Upload your Excel

Tap + → Files and pick your .xlsx or .csv. A Google Sheet works too: pick it from Drive.

03

Step 3: Paste the prompt and send

Copy The prompt above, paste it and send. Gemini writes the code and opens your dashboard in Canvas. Tap Preview if you only see code.

04

Step 4: Share it with your team

Tap Share in Canvas to get a link anyone can open on their phone. No file to send, no app to install.

Option B · ChatGPT

05

Step 5: Upload your Excel to ChatGPT

Open chatgpt.com or the ChatGPT app, start a new chat, tap + and add your file. The free plan has a daily limit on uploads, so if it says you've hit it, try again later.

06

Step 6: Paste the prompt, then open it in canvas

Send the prompt. If you only get code, reply: “Put it in canvas and show the preview.” Then tap Preview on the canvas to see your dashboard.

07

Step 7: Save and send it

Ask: “Give me the full HTML file to download.” Save the .html file and send it on WhatsApp or email. It opens in any browser, even offline.

Change it in plain words

  • “Use our brand colour #1D6F42 and add the title Q3 Project Plan.”
  • “Make it dark mode.”
  • “Add a filter by owner.”
  • “Make the cards bigger for phones.”

Good to know

  • Clean the sheet first: one header row, no merged cells, no blank rows in between.
  • Big files work better as .csv. Keep it under a few thousand rows on the free plan.
  • Don't upload client or private data you're not allowed to share. Delete those columns first, or replace names with A, B, C.
  • Always check a few numbers against your sheet before you send the dashboard.
