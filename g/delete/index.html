Why not the Claude trick?

I tested the viral "Claude deletes you from the internet" prompt on myself and a friend. Claude found no site with our details. It said those people-search sites mostly cover the US.

In India the real problem is spam and scam calls. These 4 steps are free, official, and need no AI.

Full steps

01

Step 1: Turn on full DND (1909)

Call 1909 from your phone and choose full block. Or install the official TRAI DND app and set it there. It can take up to 7 days to start working.

OTPs, bank alerts and personal calls still come through.

Still getting promo calls? Report them in the TRAI DND app, or SMS the number and date to 1909, within 3 days.

02

Step 2: Unlist from Truecaller

Use the Truecaller app? First open it and go to Settings → Privacy Center → Deactivate.

Then open truecaller.com/unlisting, enter your number with +91, solve the CAPTCHA and tap Unlist. It usually goes within 24 hours.

03

Step 3: Report scams on Chakshu

Got a fake KYC, loan, "your SIM will be blocked" or police call? Go to sancharsaathi.gov.in and open Chakshu (report suspected fraud communication).

Pick call, SMS or WhatsApp, add the details, verify with OTP and submit. Report within 30 days.

04

Step 4: Lost money? Call 1930

Call 1930 (national cyber crime helpline) immediately, or file at cybercrime.gov.in. Then call your bank to block the card or account.

The sooner you report, the better the chance of stopping the money.

Points to be noted

  • Nothing can delete you from the whole internet. These steps cut the calls you actually get.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN or screen with a caller, even if they say they're from your bank or the police.
  • Don't paste your phone number or email into the Claude prompt. Your name and city are enough.
  • All 4 steps are free and official. Ignore anyone who asks for a fee to "remove your number".
