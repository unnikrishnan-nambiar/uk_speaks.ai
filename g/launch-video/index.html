03
Review and refine

      

        
Tip 1
Watch it twice first.
Once for the story, once for the timing, before you ask for changes.

        
Tip 2
One change per message.
For example, “Make the caption in scene 2 fade at 11.5s.” Small requests come back exactly right.

        
Tip 3
Give real numbers.
Placeholder metrics look generic. Paste your actual figures.

        
Tip 4
Name the scene and the second.
“At 4s the receipt appears too fast” is clearer than “it feels rushed”.

        
Tip 5
Listen with headphones.
Check the opening build and the move into the ending. Ask for the music to be regenerated if either feels off.

        
Tip 6
Ask for a dark version.
Claude can copy the video into a second file, so you keep both.

        Note
