A 30-second launch reel for Lastbite, a surplus-food app. It follows the same steps: problem, product in action, results, how to start, then the logo and a clear call to action, with music. Tap to play with sound.

1 · Video prompt

Make a 30-second animated launch video for my product. My logo is attached — use it exactly as provided. PRODUCT Name: [PRODUCT NAME] Pitch: [ONE-LINE PITCH] For: [WHO IT IS FOR] LOOK Light, monochrome. 1920×1080, 60fps. One font, generous whitespace, smooth eased motion. No stock imagery, emoji or gradients. STORY (5 scenes) 1. Problem (0–5s): [THE PROBLEM IN ONE SENTENCE] Show it visually, with tension building. 2. Product in action (5–15s): [ONE REAL MOMENT, STEP BY STEP] Caption: "[ONE-LINE PITCH]". Only one caption on screen at a time. 3. Results (15–20s): a dashboard that counts up these numbers, with simple grid lines: [3–4 REAL NUMBERS] 4. Setup (20–26s): three concrete steps, ticked off one by one: [STEP 1], [STEP 2], [STEP 3] 5. Ending (26–30s): logo centred, then "[CALL TO ACTION]". CONTROLS Add tweaks to toggle captions and to edit the call-to-action text. BEFORE YOU BUILD Ask me questions first. If anything is still in [brackets], unclear or missing (like my brand colours, tone, or the one thing viewers must remember), ask me one question at a time, at most 5. Then show me a short plan of the 5 scenes and wait for me to say "go".

No time to fill it in?

I want a 30-second animated launch video for my product. My logo is attached — use it exactly as provided. Before building anything, interview me. Ask one question at a time, and wait for my answer: product name, one-line pitch, who it's for, the problem it solves, one real moment where the product solves it, 3–4 real numbers, 3 setup steps, and the call to action. When you have everything, show me the full brief and a 5-scene plan: problem (0–5s), product in action (5–15s), results dashboard (15–20s), setup steps (20–26s), logo and call to action (26–30s). Wait for me to say "go", then build it: light, monochrome, 1920×1080, 60fps, one font, smooth eased motion, no stock imagery, emoji or gradients.

Example: filled in

Step by step

01

Step 1: Where to place the prompt

Open Claude (the app or claude.ai) and start a new project, or a new chat. Attach your logo as a PNG with a transparent background. Paste the video prompt, replace every [bracket] with your product, and send it. Keep each answer short, because every line becomes text on screen.

Claude asks you questions back before it builds anything: anything you left in [brackets], your colours, your tone. Answer one at a time. It then shows a 5-scene plan, and you reply “go”. Using the “No time to fill it in?” prompt instead? Claude asks you every question, one by one.

02

Step 2: Add music and sound

When the first version is ready, send this in the same chat. It adds an original music track and small sound effects.

2 · Audio prompt

Add sound to the video. Generate an original music track: tension in the opening, then steady tempo and level to the end, with no drop between scenes. Add soft chimes on key actions (each product action, each setup step, the logo) and key-tap sounds wherever text is typed. Add a toggle to turn the music on and off.

03

Step 3: Iterate till you like it

  • Watch it twice: once for the story, once for the timing.
  • Ask for one change per message. Name the scene and the second: “At 4s the receipt appears too fast.”
  • Use your real numbers. Placeholder numbers look generic.
  • Listen with headphones. If the music feels off, ask Claude to regenerate it.
04

Step 4: Want a voiceover? Connect ElevenLabs

Optional. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors, choose ElevenLabs and sign in with your ElevenLabs account. No API key needed. Then send this prompt.

3 · Voiceover prompt

Write a short voiceover script for this video, one line per scene, timed to fit each scene. Show me the script first. When I say yes, use ElevenLabs to generate it with a [warm, confident] voice, add it to the video so each line starts with its scene, and lower the music while the voice is speaking.

05

Step 5: Iterate again

Watch it with the voice. Change one thing at a time: “Make the voice slower in scene 3”, “Try a different voice”, or “Move the line in scene 2 to start at 6s”.

06

Step 6: Download the video

In the video preview, click Export video and choose 1080p. This saves an MP4 with picture only. The sound comes in the next step.

07

Step 7: Combine it with the audio

Ask Claude to make a small page that joins your video and audio into one file. Open that page, choose your MP4 and click Combine. Keep the tab open in Chrome until it finishes.

4 · Combine prompt

The video export saves picture only. Build me a small page that takes my exported MP4, adds the music track, sound effects and voiceover in sync from 0:00, and downloads one MP4 with picture and sound.

08

Step 8: Download the final video

You get one MP4 with picture and sound. Play it once on your phone before posting. Prefer an app? Add the audio in CapCut or iMovie at 0:00 instead.

09

Step 9: Share it on Instagram with a link

  • Post it as a Reel. Write one line on the problem it solves, then the call to action.
  • Links in captions aren't clickable, so put your link in your bio and say “link in bio”.
  • Share it to your Story too, and add a link sticker that goes straight to your page.
  • Or ask people to comment a keyword, and send them the link by DM.
10

Step 10: Let the interest decide

Give it 3–7 days, then look at the comments, DMs, saves, shares and link clicks.

  • Lots of interest: people want it. Build it, or open a waitlist.
  • Some interest: talk to the people who replied, then try a sharper pitch.
  • Little interest: change the problem or the audience, and make a new video. It only takes one prompt.
