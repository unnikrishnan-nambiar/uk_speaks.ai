Why it matters

People now ask ChatGPT things like “Best interior designer in Malappuram?” and pick from the answer. A claimed business can show its own details there: website, photos, what you offer.

What you get, free

  • A business profile in ChatGPT: website, description, category, logo, cover photo and policies.
  • Insights for the last 28 days: how often ChatGPT mentioned you, profile views, website clicks and the topics people found you through.
  • A blue tick when you verify your ID.

Full steps

01

Step 1: Open the Business Hub

Go to chatgpt.com/business-hub (or open Businesses in the ChatGPT sidebar) and sign in to your ChatGPT account. It's in beta, so it may take a moment to show up for you.

02

Step 2: Search, then claim or create

Type your business name. If it shows up, tap it and choose Claim. If it doesn't, tap Create and add it. Then tap Get started.

03

Step 3: Verify it's yours

Pick the easiest option:

  • Business phone: get a code by SMS or call.
  • Business email: an email on your website's domain.
  • Domain (DNS): add the TXT record it shows you at your domain provider, then tap Verify domain.
  • Shopify: connect your store.

After that, OpenAI reviews your claim.

04

Step 4: Fill your profile

Add your website, category, logo, cover photo and policies. Write a clear description with what you do and where, e.g. “Interior designer in Malappuram for 2BHK flats and villas.” Use the prompt below to write it.

Prompt 1 · Business description

Write a 2–3 sentence business description for my ChatGPT business profile. Business name: [NAME]. What we do: [SERVICES]. City and area: [CITY]. Who it's for: [CUSTOMERS]. What makes us different: [USP]. Keep it factual and in plain English. No hype, no emojis, no claims I haven't given you. Then give me 5 short phrases customers might type when looking for a business like mine.

05

Step 5: Get the blue tick, then check insights

Verify your ID (done through Persona) to get the verified badge. Then open Insights once a week to see mentions, profile views, website clicks and the topics people found you through.

Points to be noted

  • It's a beta, so menus and features may change.
  • Claiming is free, but it doesn't guarantee a higher spot in answers or more sales.
  • A website click in Insights doesn't always mean the person reached your site.
  • The business agent (it answers customers for you) is only for some eligible businesses right now. Join the waitlist if you're offered one.
  • Ads in ChatGPT are separate and paid.
  • Don't confuse it with ChatGPT Business, which is OpenAI's paid team plan.
