Before you start, you need

  • Claude Pro plan. The paid Claude plan. This guide is tested on Pro.
  • A debit or credit card for Higgsfield’s free trial. Use one with a small limit.
  • A Google, Apple or Microsoft account, or an email, to sign in to Higgsfield.
  • claude.ai in a browser (phone or laptop) or the Claude desktop app.
Read this first
  • The free trial needs a card. When it ends, it turns into a paid plan automatically unless you cancel.
  • Cancel the auto-renewal right after you start the trial. You still get all 72 hours. How to cancel is in step 05 below.

How it works

  1. You type in Claude“Make a 5-second video of…”
  2. Claude writes a better promptand picks a video model
  3. Higgsfield makes the videoon its own servers
  4. The video comes back to your chatwatch it, download it, ask for changes

What’s MCP? A plug for Claude. It lets Claude use another app, here Higgsfield, from inside your chat. Claude calls it a connector.

What’s Higgsfield? An AI video and image app. Inside, it has many top video models in one place.

Connect it (one time)

01

Step 1: Open Claude’s Connectors page

Open claude.ai in your browser or the Claude desktop app. On your phone, open claude.ai in Chrome or Safari.

Go to Settings › Customize › Connectors. Tap + Add and choose Add custom connector.

02

Step 2: Paste the Higgsfield link

In the box that opens, type a name, like Higgsfield. In the second box, paste this link. Then tap Continue.

Connector link

https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp

03

Step 3: Sign in to Higgsfield and allow

Tap Connect. A Higgsfield page opens. Sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft or your email. If you don't have a Higgsfield account, this makes one.

Higgsfield asks to let Claude use your account. Read it and tap Continue. You come back to Claude and see Connected to Higgsfield.

Make your first video

04

Step 4: Ask Claude for a video

Start a new chat. Copy the prompt below and change the last line to your idea. You can type in Manglish too.

The first time, Higgsfield may show its plans and the free trial. Read which plan the trial turns into and the price, before you add your card.

Prompt 1 · First video

Use Higgsfield to make a short cinematic video, vertical 9:16, about 5 seconds. Before you make it: 1. Write a better, detailed prompt from my idea and show it to me 2. Tell me which model you'll use and how many credits it costs 3. Wait for my OK My idea: [for example: a steaming cup of chai on a rainy Kerala balcony, slow camera push-in, golden evening light]

Ideas to try

  • Small business: a 5-second ad for your shop, cake, saree or café.
  • Festival: an Onam or Vishu greeting with a pookalam or kani, no real faces.
  • Story: one scene from a short story, like a boat on the backwaters at sunrise.
  • Reels: B-roll clips for your own videos.

Not happy? Just reply: “Make it slower”, “Change to night”, “Make it 16:9”. Each new version uses credits.

05

Step 5: Cancel the auto-renewal (do it on day 1)

As soon as your trial starts, send Claude the prompt below in the same chat. Higgsfield says this stops the upcoming charge, and you keep using the trial until it ends.

Prompt 2 · Cancel

Cancel my Higgsfield trial auto-renewal. Then show my Higgsfield plan and tell me the exact date and time my trial ends.

Then double-check

  • Take a screenshot of the reply that says auto-renewal is cancelled.
  • Log in at higgsfield.ai and check your plan or billing page says it won’t renew.
  • Check your email for a cancellation message from Higgsfield.
  • After the trial ends, check your bank SMS. If you see a charge you didn’t want, contact Higgsfield support with your screenshot.

Be careful

Money
  • It’s free only for 72 hours. After that it renews into a paid plan automatically, unless you cancelled.
  • How much? On 10 November, Higgsfield’s prices were: Plus $49 a month (or $39 a month billed yearly), Ultra $129 a month (or $99 a month billed yearly). “Billed yearly” can mean the whole year is charged at once. Check what your trial screen says.
  • Set a phone alarm for 2 days after you start, even if you already cancelled. Don’t wait for the last hour.
  • Use a card with a small limit. Never share your card number, CVV or OTP in the chat. Enter card details only on the payment page.
  • “Unlimited” has limits. There may be caps on how much you can make or how many videos run at once.
  • After the trial, every video costs credits again.
Your privacy
  • Don’t upload personal photos: family photos, kids, your ID card, Aadhaar, documents. What you send goes to Higgsfield’s servers.
  • Claude itself warns: only use connectors from developers you trust. The connector can see what you ask it to make, and your email address.
  • Done with it? Go to Settings › Customize › Connectors, open Higgsfield, tap ··· and remove it.
Be honest
  • Label AI videos. When you post, write “Made with AI”. Instagram also has an AI label you can switch on.
  • No fake videos of real people. Never make a politician, celebrity, friend or relative say or do something they didn’t. It’s wrong, it fools people, and it can be illegal.
  • Don’t use other brands’ logos or ads as if they’re yours.

Do I need Claude’s top model?

No. The connector works with any Claude model. My model menu shows Fable, Claude’s top model, “included until November 16” on my Pro plan, and says it’s in Pro “for a limited time”. Nice to have, not needed.

I tested this on Claude Pro, on a Mac, in Claude’s app. Menu names can change as Claude updates.
