02

Claude chat, step 2: Use it in a chat

Start a new chat and type /li to see all 11 skills. Or just ask normally, like “write a LinkedIn post about…”, and Claude picks the right one. Start with this:

Your first post

/li-post I want to write about [YOUR IDEA]

You get three hook options, and it asks for three of your own posts. Paste the three that sound most like you. It then writes a voice.md file describing how you write.

Keep that voice: each chat starts fresh. Ask Claude for the voice.md file and add it to a Claude Project called “LinkedIn”. Every chat in that project will write like you.

The humanizer

06

Humanizer: Make any draft sound like you

/li-human is the skill that makes the rest usable. Paste any draft, from Claude or anywhere else, and it cleans and scores it before you post.

Fixed automatically

  • 113 stock AI words and phrases, like delve, leverage, robust and “in today's fast-paced world”, swapped for plain words
  • Em dashes to commas, curly quotes to straight
  • Hidden characters that sneak in when you copy and paste

Flagged for you to rewrite

  • “It's not just X, it's Y”
  • Everything in threes
  • One-word questions like “The result?”
  • Walls of hashtags and “Thoughts? Agree?” endings
  • Every sentence the same length

The five checks (0–100, higher is more human)

CheckWhat it looks at
RhythmDo your sentence lengths vary, like a person's?
SpecificsReal numbers, names and details
Stock wordsHow many AI-sounding words are left
TypographyEm dashes, curly quotes, hidden characters
VoiceContractions, “I” and “you”, natural sentences

PASS needs 70+ overall, with no single check below 55. Below that, it says REVIEW or FLAGGED and shows the lines to fix.

Use it in any chat

/li-human [PASTE YOUR DRAFT]

Example

Swapping words alone isn't enough. Getting to PASS takes a rewrite with your own real details. If it doesn't know a number, it leaves {{your number}} for you to fill in. Never post made-up numbers.

Be honest about it

The score is the humanizer's own check. It doesn't run GPTZero or any other AI detector, and nothing can promise you'll pass one. Read every post out loud before you share it.

Connect LinkedIn: Claude in Chrome

So far you paste everything in. With the Claude in Chrome extension, Claude can read LinkedIn for you: your feed, the comments on your post, your messages, your profile. It drafts, and you still press Post.

Needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher) and Chrome on a computer. It doesn't work on phones.

07

Claude in Chrome, step 1: Install the extension

  1. In Chrome, open the Chrome Web Store and search Claude. Pick the one by Anthropic.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then pin it with the puzzle-piece icon so it's always in your toolbar.
  3. Click the Claude icon and sign in with your Claude account. A side panel opens next to the page.
08

Claude in Chrome, step 2: Connect it to LinkedIn

  1. Open linkedin.com and log in as usual. Claude uses your own login. You never give it your password.
  2. Open the Claude side panel. The first time it acts on LinkedIn, it asks for permission. Allow it for linkedin.com only.
  3. Keep it on Ask before acting, so it checks with you before every click.
  4. Paste your voice.md (from step 02) into the first message, so the drafts sound like you.
09

Claude in Chrome, step 3: Let it read, you post

Open the right LinkedIn page first, then send one of these in the side panel.

Comment on your feed

I'm on my LinkedIn feed. Read the posts and pick the 3 best ones for me to comment on, from people in [YOUR FIELD]. For each, draft one short comment in my voice (my voice.md is below). Follow the /li-human rules: no stock AI words, no em dashes, real specifics. Don't post, like, follow or send anything. Just show me the drafts. I'll post the ones I like myself. [PASTE YOUR VOICE.MD]

Reply to your comments

I'm on my latest LinkedIn post. Read all the comments and draft a reply to each one that deserves it, most important first, in my voice. Show each reply under the person's name so I can paste it. Don't post anything.

Fix your profile

I'm on my LinkedIn profile. Read it, score it out of 100 like /li-profile, and rewrite my headline and About section for [WHO YOU WANT TO REACH]. Don't change anything on the page. Just show me the new text.

Keep your account safe
  • Let it read and draft. You post. LinkedIn's rules don't allow bots or automated activity, and accounts that use them can be restricted.
  • Never ask it to send connection requests or messages in bulk, like posts for you, or copy people's profiles.
  • Posts can hide instructions meant for AI. If Claude suggests something you didn't ask for, stop it and say no.

Important

In chat and Claude Code, you paste in the post, the comments or the messages. Screenshots work too. Want Claude to read LinkedIn itself? Use Claude in Chrome (steps 07–09).

Your weekly routine

  • Once a week: run /li-plan. Tell it what you do, your themes and what happened this week.
  • 10 minutes a day: /li-comment on 2–3 posts and /li-reply to your own comments.
  • Before anything goes out: run /li-human, then check every number yourself.
  • Links: put them in the first comment, not in the post.

What it can't do

  • Post, comment or send anything for you. You post every one, which keeps your account safe: auto-posting tools break LinkedIn's rules.
  • Read your feed, comments or inbox, unless you connect Claude in Chrome. Then it reads, and you still post.
  • Run on autopilot. Even with Chrome, you start every task.
Good to know

It's open source under the MIT licence, free, with no signup and no API key. The skills are plain instructions plus two small Python scripts, and neither script connects to the internet. Anthropic doesn't review outside plugins, so it's worth a quick look at the repo before you add it.
