Need help? Full steps

01

Step 1: Make it yours

Every prompt has parts in [brackets]. Swap them for your own words, numbers or product before you send, like your shop name, your city or your result.

02

Step 2: Paste it into Claude

Open claude.ai or the Claude app, start a new chat, paste the prompt and send. Claude writes the code and shows the animation as a preview next to the chat. On the phone, tap the preview card to open it.

03

Step 3: Fix it in plain words

Not quite right? Just reply: “make it slower”, “bigger text”, “use red instead of blue”, “add my logo text at the end”. Each reply updates the same animation.

04

Step 4: Turn it into a video

Open the preview full screen, tap Replay and screen-record it on your phone (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record). Trim the start and end, then add music in Instagram.

Bonus: lines that stop it looking AI-made

Add any of these to the end of a prompt:

  • “Use one accent colour only.”
  • “No emoji, no stock icons, no generic purple gradient.”
  • “Ease every movement. Nothing should move at constant speed.”
  • “Use real-looking text and numbers, not lorem ipsum.”
  • “Keep every line of text on screen for at least 1.5 seconds.”
  • “Make it feel like a premium brand film, not a template.”

Good to know

  • Results change a little every time. Run it twice and keep the better one.
  • Long prompts on the free plan can hit the daily limit. Try again later or keep chats short.
  • Only use names, logos and numbers you're allowed to use.
