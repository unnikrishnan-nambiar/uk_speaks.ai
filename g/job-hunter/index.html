What happens every day

  1. ChatGPT wakes upat the time you chose
  2. It searches 3 job sitesLinkedIn · Indeed · Naukri
  3. It removes repeatsold posts, jobs you already got, reposts
  4. It removes scamsfees, OTP requests, fake company links
  5. It checks each job against your CVwhy you fit, and what you're missing
  6. A report lands in your Gmailyou read it and apply yourself

3 words you'll see

Prompt: the message you paste into ChatGPT. It tells ChatGPT what to do.

Apify: a website with little robots that read job sites for you. You pay a few cents, from your own account.

Connect: letting ChatGPT use another app, like Gmail. You sign in yourself. ChatGPT never sees your password.

Thing 1 · Add the plugin

01

Thing 1: Add the plugin, then sign in

Do this once. Use a computer, it's easier.

  1. Make a free account at apify.com. It's the job-search robot the plugin uses.
  2. Download the AI Job Hunter plugin below. Don't unzip it.
  3. In ChatGPT, open Plugins, tap + and add the plugin file you downloaded.
  4. Sign in to Apify when it asks.
  5. Open the Gmail plugin in ChatGPT and sign in with the Gmail that should get your reports.

Thing 2 · CV + prompt

02

Thing 2: Upload your CV and paste the prompt

  1. Start a new chat in ChatGPT.
  2. Tap + and attach your CV (PDF or Word).
  3. Copy the prompt below, paste it, and send.
  4. ChatGPT asks a few questions: the job you want, the city, what time you want the email. Just answer.

The prompt

Set up AI Job Hunter using my CV and preferences. Search LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri daily at my chosen time. Require verified posting evidence within the preceding 24 hours. For the strict shortlist, require a verified numeric applicant count below 25. Show broader matches separately. Exclude previously emailed jobs and recognizable reposts across all days. Explain my fit and material gaps. Use only facts from my CV. Keep Apify Actor charges capped at $0.06 per run. Run a preview here, then create the daily task with my CV facts and these rules. I authorize report emails only to my verified own Gmail address. Confirm the schedule, timezone and recipient. Never apply or contact employers.

What the prompt tells ChatGPT, in plain words

  • Fresh jobs only: posted in the last 24 hours.
  • Top list: fewer than 25 people have applied. Everything else goes in a second list.
  • No repeats: never the same job twice, even if it's posted again.
  • Scam check: jobs that ask for money, OTPs or bank details, or look fake, are kept out.
  • Honest: it uses only what's really in your CV.
  • Cheap: never more than 6 cents a day.
  • Safe: it emails only you. It never applies or contacts anyone.

+Something went wrong?Quick fixes
  • “No Apify tools”: turn on the Apify plugin in that chat and sign in again.
  • No jobs in the top list: normal on quiet days. Look at the second list.
  • Jobs that don't fit: job sites mix in wrong roles. Read the “Missing” line.
  • No email: check Spam. Then ask ChatGPT: “Did today's AI Job Hunter email send?”
  • Stop it: tell ChatGPT “Pause my AI Job Hunter task”.
+For tech peopleThe exact job robots and limits it uses
  • Apify Actors: valig/linkedin-jobs-scraper (30 results, $0.03 cap), valig/indeed-jobs-scraper (10, $0.02), valig/naukri-jobs-scraper (10, $0.01). 120-second timeout each.
  • These are third-party tools, not official LinkedIn, Indeed or Naukri apps. Job sites can change and block them.
  • LinkedIn's “Be among the first 25 applicants” is a label, not a real count, so it doesn't pass the top list. Unknown counts don't pass either.
  • Repeats are found by checking your Gmail Sent folder for earlier AI Job Hunter emails.
  • Scam check: each job gets one label: “High-risk indicators — excluded”, “Needs verification — withheld” or “No obvious red flags found”. The last one is not a guarantee. Excluded jobs are listed separately with the reason.
  • Tested on 9 November 2026: all 3 job sites returned results and a test email arrived. A full scheduled run with all 3 sites wasn't verified yet.
Stay safe
  • Never type a password or Apify token into the chat. Sign in through the plugin only.
  • It only finds jobs. You read them and apply yourself.
  • Check every job link before you apply. Scrapers can get things wrong.
  • A real job never asks you to pay a fee or share an OTP. If one does, walk away.
