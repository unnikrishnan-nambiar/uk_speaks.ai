Step 1 · Your content file

PRESENTATION: [Title of your talk] AUDIENCE: [who is watching, e.g. my manager / clients / class] GOAL: [what they should do or decide after it] LENGTH: [number of slides, e.g. 8] SLIDE 1: [Title slide] - [Your name, date] SLIDE 2: [The problem] - [point] - [point] - [a number or fact, if you have one] SLIDE 3: [Your idea / solution] - [point] - [point] SLIDE 4: [How it works] - [step 1] - [step 2] - [step 3] SLIDE 5: [Proof: result, example or data] - [point] SLIDE 6: [Next steps / ask] - [what you want them to do] NOTES: [anything else: tone, words to avoid, data to highlight]

Step 2 · Free design websites

  • SlidesCarnival (slidescarnival.com): Free, no sign-up. PowerPoint and Google Slides.
  • Slidesgo (slidesgo.com): Free templates need credit to Slidesgo; free accounts have a monthly download limit.
  • Microsoft Create (create.microsoft.com): Free PowerPoint templates from Microsoft.
  • AllPPT (allppt.com): Direct .pptx downloads. Lots of ads, so click carefully.

Download the PowerPoint (.pptx) version. If it comes as a .zip, unzip it first and upload the .pptx inside.

Step 3 · The prompt

I've uploaded two files: 1. A PowerPoint template (.pptx): use its design ONLY. 2. My content file: use its content ONLY. Build a new presentation as a downloadable .pptx file. Design rules: - First, look at every slide layout in the template and list them (title, section, text, two-column, image, etc.). - Use the template's fonts, colours, backgrounds and layouts. Don't invent a new theme. - Pick a DIFFERENT layout that fits each slide's content. Don't repeat the same layout on every slide. - Delete the template's sample slides and placeholder text, and any image you don't use. Story rules: - Keep my slide order unless a different order clearly tells the story better. If you change it, tell me why. - Use short headlines that state the point ("Sales grew 30%", not "Sales"). - Max 4 bullets per slide, max 8 words per bullet. Move extra detail into the speaker notes. - Don't add facts or numbers that aren't in my content file. Before building: show me the slide-by-slide plan (slide → layout → headline). Then build the .pptx.

Need help? Full steps

01

Step 1: Fill in the content file

Copy the content file above into Notes, Word or Google Docs. Replace everything in [brackets] with your own words, then save it as a .txt, .docx or PDF. Clear content gives a clear story. Write the point of each slide, not just a topic.

02

Step 2: Pick and download a design

Open any of the sites above, choose a template you like and download the PowerPoint (.pptx) file. Pick one with lots of different slide layouts. More layouts means more variety in your deck.

03

Step 3: Upload both files to Claude

Go to claude.ai or the Claude app, start a new chat, tap + and add both files: the template .pptx and your content file. Creating files works on the free plan too.

04

Step 4: Paste the prompt and approve the plan

Paste The prompt and send. Claude first shows a slide-by-slide plan. Reply “looks good, build it”, or ask for changes. Then it builds the deck and gives you a .pptx to download.

05

Step 5: Open, check, present

Open the file in PowerPoint, Google Slides or Keynote. Read every slide once: AI can still put text in the wrong box. Small fixes are faster by hand. For bigger ones, send the prompt below.

Something looks off? Send this

Some slides look crowded or off-template. Recheck every slide against the template: same fonts and colours, no text overflowing, no leftover placeholder text. Fix them and give me the updated .pptx.

Tip

Not only Claude: the same files and prompt also work in other AI apps that can create PowerPoint files, like ChatGPT. Results differ, so try two and keep the better one.

Good to know

  • Free plans have daily limits. A long deck can use a lot of it, so start with 6–8 slides.
  • Templates with very complex designs (lots of shapes and animations) can confuse the AI. Simple, clean templates work best.
  • Slidesgo's free templates ask you to credit them: keep their credit slide.
  • Don't upload confidential company data you aren't allowed to share.
