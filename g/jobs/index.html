The daily job task

Every morning, search the web for [JOB TITLE] jobs in [CITY, or Remote] posted in the last 24 hours. Look on LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Bayt and GulfTalent. My profile: [paste 4–6 lines from your CV: current role, years of experience, main skills, tools, languages]. Don't show me: [for example: jobs under ₹30,000 or AED 5,000 a month, night shifts, sales roles]. Pick the 5 jobs that fit my profile best. For each one, give the job title, company, location, link, when it was posted, one line on why I fit, and one gap I should prepare for. For the best match, write a short cover note I can send. Put anything you don't know about me, like numbers or results, in [brackets]. Don't make them up. Skip any job that asks for a fee, a deposit or a visa payment, or that looks like a scam.

What happens every morning

  1. Your profilesaved inside the task
  2. Daily taskruns on its own
  3. Web searchjobs from the last 24 hours
  4. Top 5with why you fit
  5. Emailplus a cover note

Need help? Full steps

01

Step 1: Write your profile

Open your CV and copy 4–6 lines: your current role, years of experience, main skills and tools, languages, and the city you want. A scheduled task can't read a CV file, so the text has to be inside the prompt. More real detail gives better matches.

02

Step 2: Turn on email notifications

In ChatGPT, open Settings → Notifications and turn on email (and push, if you want it on your phone) for tasks. That's how the 5 jobs reach your inbox.

03

Step 3: Create the task

Start a new chat, paste the prompt with your details filled in, and send it. ChatGPT confirms it as a scheduled task. You'll find it under Scheduled in the menu. On the free plan, it runs once a day in a morning window. Paid plans can pick an exact time, like 6:00 AM.

04

Step 4: Test it today

Don't want to wait till tomorrow? Paste the same prompt in a normal chat without "Every morning". You'll see the kind of results to expect. Open every link and check the jobs are real.

05

Step 5: Apply fast, apply yourself

ChatGPT finds and sorts the jobs. You still apply. Fill in the [brackets] in the cover note with your real details, and apply the same morning. New jobs get many applicants within hours.

Upgrade: 3 times a day (paid plans)

Change "Every morning" in the task to: Every day at 6 AM, 12 PM and 6 PM. Only show jobs you haven't sent me before. If there are no new ones, say "No new jobs" in one line.

Points to be noted

  • The free plan allows 3 active tasks, each running at most once a day. Exact times and more runs need a paid plan.
  • Web search won't find every job. LinkedIn listings don't always show up. Keep checking the big job sites yourself too.
  • Open every link before you apply. If a link is broken or the job looks old, skip it.
  • Never pay to get a job. No real company asks for a fee, deposit or visa payment to hire you.
  • Don't let the cover note invent results. Replace every [bracket] with something true.
  • Menus in ChatGPT change often. If you can't find a setting, ask ChatGPT: "How do I turn on task notifications?"
