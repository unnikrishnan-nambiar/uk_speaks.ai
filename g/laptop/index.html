01 · Word document from your notes · PAID

Turn these rough notes into a clean Word document with a title, headings and bullet points. Keep my meaning, fix the language, and give me the .docx file to download. [paste your notes]

02 · Slides from your outline · PAID

Make a 6-slide presentation from this outline. One idea per slide, short bullet points, a simple clean design, and speaker notes for each slide. Give me the file to download. [paste your outline]

03 · Bank statement → monthly expense sheet · PAID

Read this bank statement PDF. Make a spreadsheet of every expense with date, description, amount and a category (Food, Fuel, Bills, Shopping, EMI, Other). Add a summary tab with the total per category and a pie chart. Give me the .xlsx file.

04 · Invoice or quotation PDF · PAID

Make a professional quotation PDF. My business: [name, address, phone]. Customer: [name]. Items: [item, qty, price]. Add 18% GST, the total in figures and words, and 7-day validity. Clean, simple design.

05 · Charts and insights from your numbers · PAID

Here is my data. Make a spreadsheet with clear charts (monthly trend and top 5 items) and write 5 simple insights about what is going up, what is going down and what I should do next. [paste your numbers or upload the Excel/CSV]

06 · Resume tailored to a job post · FREE TO TRY

Here is my resume and a job post. Rewrite my resume for this job: match the keywords, put the most relevant experience first, keep it to one page and true to my actual experience. Give me a PDF. [upload resume] [paste job post]

07 · Photo of a page → editable text · FREE TO TRY

Read this photo of a printed page and type out the full text exactly. Keep the headings and layout, and give it to me as an editable Word file. Mark anything you can't read as [unclear].

08 · Photo and signature resized for forms · PAID

Resize this photo to 200×230 pixels, white background, under 50 KB, as a JPG for an online application form. Then do the same for my signature at 140×60 pixels under 20 KB. Give me both files.

09 · Long PDF → simple Malayalam summary · FREE TO TRY

Read this PDF and explain it in simple Malayalam in 8-10 points. Then list any dates, amounts, deadlines or things I must do. Keep the English terms in English.

10 · Research before you buy · FREE TO TRY

I want to buy [product] under ₹[budget]. Research the best 4 options available in India right now. Compare price, key specs, reviews and after-sales service in a table, then give me one clear pick and why. Include links to your sources.

11 · A website you can share with a link · PAID

Make a simple one-page website for [my bakery / my event / my portfolio]. Include [menu / timings / map / photos / contact], a WhatsApp button to [number], and a clean mobile-friendly design. Publish it and give me the link.

12 · Raw video → edited reel · PAID

Edit this video into a vertical reel. Cut the pauses and mistakes, add clean English subtitles, zoom in on key moments, and add simple text on screen for the main points. Keep it under 60 seconds and give me the final MP4.

Limitations

  • 8 of the 12 need a paid plan. 6, 7, 9 and 10 can be tried on free.
  • Video takes time. A full edit can take 45-60 minutes. Start it before you leave.
  • Small tweaks are slower on a phone. Ask for changes in plain words instead of editing yourself.
  • Check before you send. Numbers, names, dates and research can have mistakes.
  • Hide private details. Blur account numbers and ID numbers before uploading statements or documents.
  • Needs internet. Your files are made on Claude's computer, so download what you want to keep.
