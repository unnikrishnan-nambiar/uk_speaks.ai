What makes her different

  • Listens while she talks. Most AI avatars wait for you to finish, think, then reply. Nora reacts while you're still speaking.
  • Nods, smiles, looks away like a person on a video call.
  • Sees you. With the camera on she can describe you and what you show her.
  • 89% vs 35%: in Nuance Labs' own test her reply timing matched real people 89% of the time. The next best system got 35%. This is their test, not an independent one.
  • Her face and voice come from a real actress, used with permission.

Try 1 · Can she see you?

Can you see me? Describe what I am wearing and what is behind me.

Try 2 · Interrupt her

Tell me a long story about Kerala. (Then interrupt her halfway with a question and see how she reacts.)

Try 3 · Mock interview

Be my interviewer for a [job role] job. Ask me one question at a time, react to my answers, and give me honest feedback at the end.

Try 4 · Spoken English practice

Help me practise spoken English. Talk to me like a friend, and when I make a mistake, gently correct me and make me say it again.

API

Build your own with it

The Nuplex API is in beta for developers at platform.nuancelabs.ai. You sign up, write a persona prompt, and put a live avatar in your own app or website.

Ideas: a receptionist on your website, an interview coach, a language tutor. Pricing isn't published yet, so check after you sign up.

Points to be noted

  • English only for now.
  • She can end a call early as a safety measure, and she can't sing.
  • The API doesn't allow cloning real people, adult content, or healthcare and under-18 use cases.
  • She can get details wrong about what she sees. Don't rely on her for anything important.
  • Don't show IDs, bank cards or anything private on camera.
