What can it do?All the features, in one list
  • Multi-track timeline with keyframes and audio waveforms for exact cuts.
  • Colour grading: colour wheels, curves, log conversion, and your own LUTs.
  • Your own fonts: import font files.
  • Instagram's music library: licensed, royalty-free tracks, plus hundreds of sound and video effects.
  • Unlimited auto captions in many languages, which you can edit and style.
  • Phone styles sync: audio and text styles you saved in the phone app show up on desktop.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: set your own or bring the ones from another editor.
  • Any shape: vertical reels and horizontal videos.
  • 4K export, no watermark.
  • Post straight to Instagram with tags, location and trial reels, or download the video for other apps.

Need help? Full steps

01

Step 1: Check your Mac

Click the Apple menu (top left) → About This Mac. Look at “Chip”. If it says Apple M1, M2, M3 or newer, you're good. Older Intel Macs, Windows and Linux don't work yet.

02

Step 2: Download and install

Open edits.instagram.com/desktop. Click Get Edits for Mac. Open the file you downloaded. Follow the steps on screen. It's free.

03

Step 3: Sign in and make a video

Sign in with your Instagram account. Start a new project (a new video). Drag your clips in. Cut and arrange them on the timeline. When you're done, save it in 4K or post it to Instagram.

Extra: let an AI edit for youOptional. For people who already use AI helpers.

What's MCP? A plug that lets an AI helper (an “agent”) use an app's buttons for you.

Edits for Mac has this plug. So you can ask an AI agent to make a rough first cut of your video. Then you finish it by hand.

  1. Your videoe.g. in Downloads
  2. AI agentyou say “edit this”
  3. Edits MCPthe agent uses Edits' tools
  4. Rough cutsaved as an Edits project
  5. You finishtweak, then post

How to turn it on: In Edits, open Settings. Turn on MCP. Then connect an AI agent that supports MCP. Instagram's own example is Meta's agent, Muse.

Copy this message and send it to your agent:

Prompt for your AI agent

Use the Edits MCP to make a rough cut of [VIDEO NAME] in my Downloads folder. Make it a vertical reel under [60] seconds. Cut long pauses, repeated lines and mistakes, and keep the best take of each line. Save it as a new Edits project so I can finish it myself. Don't publish or post anything. When you're done, tell me what you cut.

Points to be noted

  • Mac only for now, and it needs an M1 chip or newer. No Windows version has been announced yet.
  • You can't yet open the same project on your phone and your Mac. Instagram says this is coming.
  • Instagram plans weekly updates, so menus and features may change.
  • Connecting Claude Code (an AI helper) to Edits isn't officially documented yet. Comment “TUTORIAL” on the reel if you want a full step-by-step.
  • Let the agent make the rough cut, but always check the video yourself before you post.
