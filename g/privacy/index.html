What happened

  • In July 2026 a Reddit post showed that searching site:claude.ai/share on Google brought up chats people had shared, some with health records and office documents.
  • Only chats shared with a link were exposed. Private chats were not.
  • Making a link private again doesn't remove copies other sites may have saved, so check now and share carefully from here on.
01

Step 1: Make shared chats private

Open Claude → your name → Settings → Privacy. Open Shared chats and Shared artifacts. Unshare each chat, and set each artifact's visibility to Private.

Anyone who has a share link can read the whole chat, including files you uploaded.

02

Step 2: Turn off sensitive topics in memory

Settings → Memory → Include sensitive topics in memory → off. Choose Pause to keep what's already saved, or Reset to delete those memories too.

Claude will stop saving things like health or religion to memory.

03

Step 3: Turn off model training

Settings → Privacy → Help improve our AI models → off.

When it's on, new chats can be used to train Claude and kept for up to 5 years. When it's off, your chats aren't used for future training. Chats already used in training can't be taken back.

Also do this

  • Settings → Privacy → Location metadata → off.
  • For anything sensitive, use an incognito chat. It isn't used to improve Claude.
  • Never upload Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card numbers, passwords or OTPs. Crop or blur them first.
  • Office data: follow your company's AI policy and use the tool your company approves.
  • Using ChatGPT or Gemini too? The PDF has their settings.
