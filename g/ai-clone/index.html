Clone

Build your AI clone. One that writes, talks and looks like you.

  1. Teach AI who you are, onceClaude or ChatGPT project · 20 minutes
  2. Make your video avatarHeyGen · a 2-minute video of you
  3. Use your cloneInterviews, messages, posts, videos

Two clones, one you

  • Your AI clone (text): knows your work, your stories, your opinions and how you write. It drafts answers, messages and posts the way you would, using only true facts about you.
  • Your video avatar: a video version of you, with your face and voice. Give it a script and it makes a video of you saying it. No camera, no retakes.

The text clone writes. The avatar presents. You check everything before anyone sees it.

Part 1 · Your AI clone (text)

01

Step 1: Make a project and add your files

In Claude or ChatGPT, create a project called “My AI clone”. Add what describes you:

  • Your CV and your LinkedIn profile as a PDF (on a computer: your profile → More → Save to PDF).
  • Things you wrote: 5–10 emails, posts, captions or messages. This is how it learns your style.
  • Optional: appraisals, project notes, your business's website or brochure.

Remove phone numbers, addresses and anything confidential first.

02

Step 2: Let it interview you, once

Start a chat in the project and send Prompt 1. It asks you questions one at a time, for about 20 minutes. Answer by typing or by voice. The more real detail you give, the more it sounds like you.

Prompt 1 · Build my clone

I want to build an "AI clone" of myself: an AI that knows my work and life story and writes the way I do. Read my files in this project first. Then interview me to fill in what they don't say. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Ask follow-ups until you have real details: what I did myself, the numbers, the result. Cover: - Who I am and what I do, in my own words - My career or business story: each step, and why - 8–10 real stories: a big win, a failure, a conflict, a tight deadline, a difficult customer, learning something fast, a hard decision - What I'm good at, with proof, and what I'm still working on - What I believe about my work: 3–5 opinions I'd defend - How I talk and write: formal or casual, short or long, words and phrases I use, words I never use, emoji or not - The languages I use, and when Never invent anything. If I'm unsure of a number, write [CHECK]. When you have enough, write my CLONE FILE: 1. About me (5 lines, in my voice) 2. Timeline (role or business, dates, one-line impact) 3. Story bank: each story with a title, tags, and Situation / Task / Action / Result, with numbers 4. Strengths with proof, and what I'm working on 5. My opinions 6. How I talk and write, with 3 short examples 7. Facts not to share (anything I mark as private) Give it to me as a downloadable file called my-clone.md, and also in the chat.

03

Step 3: Save the clone file and switch it on

  1. Download my-clone.md. Read it, fix anything wrong, and fill every [CHECK].
  2. Add it to the project's files.
  3. Paste Prompt 2 into the project's Instructions. Every chat in this project is now your clone.

Prompt 2 · Project instructions

You are my AI clone. my-clone.md and my other files in this project are the only truth about me. - Write and answer as me, in the first person, in the style described in "How I talk and write". - Use only facts from my files. Never invent a job, skill, number, story or opinion. - If my files don't cover something, say "My clone file doesn't cover this yet", ask me, and tell me what to add to my-clone.md. - Never share anything listed under "Facts not to share". - Everything you write is a draft for me to check. Keep it short unless I ask for more. Talk to me in [English / Manglish]. Write in the language I ask for.

Test it

Ask: “Introduce yourself in 30 seconds” and “What do you think about [a topic you care about]?”. If it doesn't sound like you, tell it what's off, and ask it to update my-clone.md.

Part 2 · Your video avatar

We'll use HeyGen, a popular avatar app. Its free plan includes one avatar of yourself; free videos carry a watermark and are limited each month. Synthesia and other apps work in a similar way.

  • HeyGenMake a video avatar of yourself, with your voice.
04

Step 4: Record your training video

This one video is what your avatar learns your face, voice and movement from. Get it right once.

  • One take, no cuts, 1–2 minutes. Phone camera at eye level, 1080p, on a stand.
  • Light on your face, from a window in front of you. Plain background, quiet room.
  • Look at the camera and talk naturally: read your clone's 30-second intro twice.
  • Small hand movements, no hands covering your face. Pause with your mouth closed between sentences.
  • Wear what you'd wear in the videos you'll make. The avatar keeps those clothes.
05

Step 5: Create the avatar

  1. Sign up at heygen.com. Open Avatars → New Avatar → Clone a real person.
  2. Upload your training video.
  3. Consent check: it shows a code and asks you to read it on camera. Only you can make an avatar of you; this stops anyone cloning someone else.
  4. Wait for it to process, usually under half an hour. Your voice is cloned from the same video.
06

Step 6: Make your first video

Ask your text clone for the script, then paste it into HeyGen. Pick your avatar, paste the script, generate, and watch it before you share it.

Prompt 3 · Video script

Write a [45]-second video script, as me, about: [TOPIC] For: [WHO WILL WATCH] Language: [English / Malayalam / Hindi] Rules: it will be spoken by my video avatar, so write how I talk, not how I write. Short sentences. Start with a hook in the first line. One idea only. End with: [WHAT I WANT THEM TO DO]. No facts that aren't in my clone file. Mark pauses with "...".

Make it look real

Keep scripts short: 30–60 seconds looks most natural. Spell numbers and names the way they're said (“twenty twenty-six”). If a word sounds wrong, spell it the way it sounds. Watch every video fully before sharing.

07

Use 1: Prepare for interviews

Start a new chat in the project for each job, attach the job description and send Prompt 4. Then make each answer yours: read it, fix it, and practise saying it in your own words.

Prompt 4 · Interview prep

I have an interview for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY]. The job description is attached. 1. Match the 6 most important requirements to stories from my clone file. Mark any with no story as a GAP, and tell me the honest way to handle it. 2. Predict the 12 questions I'm most likely to get, including "Tell me about yourself", "Why this company?" and a weakness question. 3. Answer each as me, in 60–90 seconds, in STAR format where it fits, using only my clone file. 4. Then ask me the questions one at a time. After each of my answers, score it out of 10 and tell me the one thing my clone's answer had that mine didn't.

Learn from your avatar: turn your 3 hardest answers into short avatar videos and watch “yourself” giving your best answer, then say it along with the video. For a full mock interview with a report, see the mock interview guide.

08

Use 2: Keep your clone up to date

After a new job, project, win or interview, tell your clone: “Here's what happened: […] Update my-clone.md.” Replace the old file with the new one. The more it knows, the better it gets.

Example: “Tell me about a failure”

Use it honestly

  • Never in a live interview or call. Not to answer for you, not to whisper answers, not as your face on camera. It's dishonest, and companies reject people for it.
  • Label avatar videos as AI. Say so in the caption, and turn on Instagram's AI info label when you post.
  • Only clone yourself. Never make an avatar or voice of anyone else.
  • Nothing made up. Every story, number and opinion must be true. You'll be asked about it.
  • Read before you send. Your clone drafts. You decide.
  • Protect your avatar. Use a strong password and 2FA on HeyGen. Delete the avatar if you stop using it.
Good to know

Projects work on the free plans of Claude and ChatGPT, with limits on files and messages. Your clone file is just a text file, so it works in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. HeyGen's free plan and limits change often: check its pricing page before you rely on it.
