Clone

What's an AI clone?

An AI that knows your career as well as you do: every job, every project, your best stories with real numbers, and how you talk. You teach it once. After that, for every new job, it answers interview questions the way you would, using only your real experience.

You read its answers, fix them, and practise them out loud until they're yours. Prep that took a weekend takes an evening.

Part 1 · Build your clone

01

Step 1: Make a project and add your files

In Claude or ChatGPT, create a new project called “My AI clone”. Add these files:

  • Your CV.
  • Your LinkedIn profile as a PDF: on your profile, tap More → Save to PDF (on a computer).
  • Optional: appraisals, awards, project notes, and a few emails or posts you wrote, so it learns how you sound.

Remove your phone number, address and anything confidential from your employer first.

02

Step 2: Let it interview you, once

Start a chat in the project and send Prompt 1. It asks about your career one question at a time, digging for the stories and numbers your CV leaves out. Answer by typing or by voice. Take about 20 minutes. Be honest: the clone is only as good as what you tell it.

Prompt 1 · Build my clone

I want to build an "AI clone" of my professional self, to prepare for job interviews. Read my files in this project first. Then interview me to fill in what my CV doesn't say. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Ask follow-ups until you have real details: what I did myself, the numbers, the result. Cover: - My career story: why I chose each job and why I left - 8–10 stories for common interview topics: a big win, a failure, a conflict, leading without authority, a tight deadline, a difficult customer or stakeholder, learning something fast, a decision I made with little data - My strengths with proof, and one or two honest weaknesses and what I'm doing about them - Skills and tools I really use, and how well - What I want next, and why - How I talk: formal or casual, short or detailed, phrases I use Never invent anything. If I'm unsure of a number, write [CHECK]. When you have enough, write my CLONE FILE in this format: 1. About me (5 lines, in my voice) 2. Career timeline (role, company, dates, one-line impact) 3. Story bank: each story with a title, topic tags, and Situation / Task / Action / Result, with numbers 4. Strengths with proof 5. Weaknesses, honestly framed 6. Skills and tools, with my level 7. What I want next 8. How I talk (tone, length, phrases) Give it to me as a downloadable file called my-clone.md, and also in the chat.

03

Step 3: Save the clone file and give it its instructions

  1. Download my-clone.md, read it, fix anything wrong, and fill every [CHECK].
  2. Add it to the project's files.
  3. Paste Prompt 2 into the project's Instructions. Now every chat in this project is your clone.

Prompt 2 · Project instructions

You are my AI clone and interview coach. my-clone.md, my CV and my other files in this project are the only truth about me. AS MY CLONE - Answer interview questions in the first person, as me, in the tone described in "How I talk". - Use only facts from my files. Never invent a job, skill, tool, number or story. - If my files don't cover something, say "My clone file doesn't cover this yet" and ask me for the details. Then tell me exactly what to add to my-clone.md. - Keep spoken answers to 60–90 seconds (about 150–200 words), in STAR format for "tell me about a time" questions. AS MY COACH - When I share a job description, match it to my story bank, and point out gaps honestly. - When I answer a question myself, compare it with my clone's answer: what I missed, what I said better, and one thing to fix. Talk to me in [English / Manglish]. Write my answers in English.

Part 2 · Prepare for a job

04

Step 4: Give it the job description

Start a new chat in the project for each job. Attach or paste the job description and send Prompt 3. You get the questions they're likely to ask, your best answer to each, and the gaps to work on.

Prompt 3 · Prep for this job

I have an interview for this job: [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY], [HR / TECHNICAL / HIRING MANAGER] round. The job description is attached (or below). 1. MATCH: list the 6 most important requirements, and the story from my clone file that proves each one. Mark any requirement I have no story for as a GAP. 2. QUESTIONS: predict the 12 questions I'm most likely to get, including "Tell me about yourself", "Why this company?", "Why are you leaving?", a weakness question and 2 tough ones about my gaps. 3. ANSWERS: answer each one as me, in my voice, using only my clone file. 60–90 seconds each. 4. GAPS: for each GAP, tell me the honest way to handle it in the interview. 5. MY QUESTIONS: 3 smart questions I can ask them at the end. [PASTE THE JOB DESCRIPTION IF NOT ATTACHED]

05

Step 5: Make the answers yours

Your clone's answers are a first draft, not a script. Read each one out loud. Anything that doesn't sound like you, tell it: “Make answer 4 simpler”, “Use the Infosys story instead”, “That number is wrong, it was 30%”. Then put it away and answer in your own words. Interviewers can tell when you're reciting.

06

Step 6: Practise: you vs your clone

Turn on voice mode in the same chat and send Prompt 4. It asks you the questions one by one. You answer first, then it shows how your clone would answer and what to fix.

Prompt 4 · You vs your clone

Let's practise. Ask me the predicted questions one at a time, like a real interviewer. Don't show your answer first. After each of my answers: - Give it a score out of 10. - Show the key points my clone's answer had that mine missed. - Give me one thing to fix, then ask the next question. After the last question, give me my 3 weakest answers to practise again tonight.

Want a full mock interview with no help in between, and a report at the end? Use the mock interview guide, and add your clone file to that project too.

07

Step 7: Make your clone smarter after every interview

Right after each interview, open the project and say: “Here are the questions they asked me today: […] Which ones did my clone file not cover? Update my-clone.md.” Replace the old file with the new one. Each interview makes the next one easier.

Example: “Tell me about a failure”

Rules for using your clone

  • For practice only. Never use it during a real interview, to answer for you or whisper answers. It's dishonest, interviewers spot it, and many companies reject you for it.
  • Nothing made up. Every story, number and skill must be true. You'll be asked follow-up questions about all of it.
  • Check every [CHECK] before you rely on an answer.
  • Keep it private. Leave out salary slips, ID numbers and your employer's confidential data.
Good to know

Projects work on the free plans of Claude and ChatGPT, with limits on files and messages. Voice mode works best in the phone app. Your clone file is just a text file: you can use the same one in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.
