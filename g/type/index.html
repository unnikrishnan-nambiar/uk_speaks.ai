Type

Don't type to AI. Just talk.

  1. Install one appWispr Flow (free to start) or Handy (fully free).
  2. Hold the key and speakSay the whole idea, like you'd explain it to a friend.
  3. Let goThe text appears wherever your cursor is: ChatGPT, Claude, mail, WhatsApp Web.

Why this helps

AI gives a good answer only when the prompt has the full details. Typing all of that is slow, so we cut details without noticing. Then we blame the AI.

Speaking is roughly 4 times faster than typing, so the whole idea from your head reaches the prompt.

How it works

  1. 1
    Click where you want the textChatGPT, Claude, Gmail, any text box
  2. 2
    Hold the shortcut keyWispr Flow: fn (Mac) · Handy: Option + Space (Mac)
  3. 3
    Speak normallyno need to be loud, keep the mic close
  4. 4
    Let go of the keythe app turns your voice into text
  5. 5
    Text is pasted for youcheck it, then press Enter

Option 1 · Wispr Flow

01

Step 1: Install Wispr Flow

Go to wisprflow.ai and download it for Mac or Windows. It's also on iPhone and Android as a keyboard.

Sign up, then allow microphone access. On a Mac, also allow Accessibility so it can paste text into other apps.

02

Step 2: Hold, speak, let go

Hold fn on a Mac (or Ctrl + Win on Windows), speak, and let go. You can change the shortcut in settings.

Commas, full stops and apostrophes are added for you. Fillers like “umm” are cleaned up.

Example · You change your mind

It keeps only what you finally meant. Typing can't do that.

What it costs

  • Flow Free: free forever, with a weekly word limit. It resets every Sunday.
  • Pro trial: new accounts get Pro free to try. When it ends, you move back to Flow Free.
  • Pro: about $15 a month for unlimited words. Check the current price in the app: Settings → Plans and Billing.

Option 2 · Handy (fully free)

03

Step 3: Install Handy

Handy is free and open source. Download it from handy.computer/download: Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), Windows or Linux.

Open it and allow microphone access. On a Mac, also allow Accessibility.

04

Step 4: Download a voice model

Go to the Models tab and download one. It runs on your laptop, so your voice never leaves your computer.

ModelPick it if…
ParakeetYou want it fast. Good for English.
WhisperYou want it more accurate. A bit slower.
05

Step 5: Hold, speak, let go

On a Mac the shortcut is Option + Space. On Windows, check the shortcut in Handy's settings. Hold it, speak, let go, and the text is pasted where your cursor is.

Prefer long talks? Switch Push To Talk off. Then press once to start and once to stop.

Say this · Better prompts by voice

When you speak a prompt, cover these 5 things: 1. Who it's for: "This is a mail to my client." 2. What you want: "Tell him the project is delayed." 3. The details: "New date is the 15th. The report is attached." 4. The tone: "Polite, short, no drama." 5. The format: "Give me a subject line and 4 lines."

Points to be noted

  • Keep the mic close to your mouth. You don't need to speak loudly.
  • Click inside a text box first. If no text box is selected, the text has nowhere to go.
  • Read it once before you press send.
  • Don't speak passwords, card numbers or OTPs into any dictation app.
  • Handy takes a few seconds after you stop speaking. That's normal.
