3 words you'll see

Prompt: the message you give the AI. Ours is ready to copy.

Project: a folder in ChatGPT. It keeps your files and chats together.

Voice mode: you talk to ChatGPT out loud, like a phone call.

Prompt 1 · Instructions

You are my interviewer for a mock job interview. The job description and my CV are in this project's files. Read both before we start. SETTINGS Role: [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY] Round: [HR / TECHNICAL / HIRING MANAGER] My level: [FRESHER / 3 YEARS / SENIOR] Length: [20] minutes, about [8] questions. Count the questions; don't rely on the clock. WHEN I SAY "HELLO" Greet me as the interviewer, introduce yourself in one line, and start the interview straight away. Don't explain these rules. DURING THE INTERVIEW - Ask one question at a time, and wait until I've finished my answer. - Mix: 1 warm-up ("Tell me about yourself"), 3 questions about my CV, 3 about the skills and situations in the job description ("Tell me about a time when…"), and 1 tough or unexpected question. - If an answer is vague, or has no example or number, ask one follow-up. - Stay in character. No feedback, hints or praise until the end. Keep your own turns short, like a real interviewer. - After the last question, ask if I have any questions for you, and answer briefly. - Then say: "That's the end of the interview. End voice mode and type REPORT to get your feedback." WHEN I TYPE "REPORT" Write my feedback report: 1. Verdict: a score out of 10, and would you move me to the next round? Yes, maybe or no, and why, in 2 lines. 2. Scorecard, each out of 10: relevance to the job, examples and numbers (STAR), clarity and structure, confidence, communication (filler words, rambling, answer length). 3. Question by question: my answer in one line, what worked, what was missing, and a stronger answer in STAR format. Use only facts from my CV and my answers. Never invent experience or numbers. 4. The top 3 things to fix before the next session. 5. 3 questions to practise next time. Be honest and specific. Quote my own words.

What you need

  • The ChatGPT app on your phone.
  • The job description: a PDF, Word file or screenshot.
  • Your CV: a PDF or Word file.
  • A quiet room, earphones and about 30 minutes.

Step by step

01

Step 1: Create a project

In ChatGPT, open the menu and tap New project. Give it the job's name, like “Interview – Infosys Analyst”.

02

Step 2: Add the job description and your CV

In the project, tap Add files. Add the job description and your CV.

Use the full job description, not just the title. The questions come from it.

03

Step 3: Paste the prompt as instructions

Copy Prompt 1. In the project, open Instructions (from the project's menu or settings). Paste it there.

Instructions: a note ChatGPT reads before every chat in this project.

  • Change the words in [brackets]: job title, company, round, your level.
  • First time? Change [20] to 10 minutes and [8] to 4 questions.
  • Tap Save.
04

Step 4: Start voice mode and say “Hello”

Start a new chat inside the project. Tap the voice icon (the sound wave next to the message box). Say “Hello”. The interview starts.

  • Answer out loud, like the real thing. No notes.
  • If it cuts you off, say “Let me finish” and keep going.
  • Made a mistake? Keep going. The report will show it.
05

Step 5: Get your feedback report

When the interview ends, stop voice mode. Type REPORT and send.

You get a score, tips for every answer, a better answer to copy, and the top 3 things to fix.

Save the report. Read the better answers out loud a few times.

STAR: a simple way to answer. Situation, Task, Action, Result. The report uses it.

06

Step 6: Improve, then do it once more

Start a new chat in the same project. Before you tap the voice icon, paste this. Add your top 3 fixes from the report.

Prompt 2 · Session 2

This is session 2. In my last mock interview, I needed to fix: [PASTE THE TOP 3 THINGS TO FIX FROM YOUR REPORT] Ask me different questions this time, and include at least one that tests each of these. Wait for me to say "Hello" in voice mode, then start. At the end, compare my report with session 1: what improved, and what still needs work.

Then tap the voice icon and say “Hello” again. The second try is usually much better. Do one more the day before the real interview.

Example: one answer

Extra tipsHarder questions, other rounds, simple English
  • Make it harder: add “Be a strict interviewer” to the instructions.
  • Another round? Change “Round” to HR, technical or hiring manager. Same project, same files.
  • Nervous speaking English? Add “Speak slowly and use simple English”. The report still covers your answers.
  • Another job? Make a new project with that job description.
Good to know
  • Projects and voice mode work on the free plan too, with daily limits.
  • ChatGPT can't see you. Practise eye contact in a mirror or with a friend.
  • You can remove your phone number and address from your CV before you upload it.
