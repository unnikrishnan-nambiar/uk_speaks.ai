Prompt 1 · Project instructions

Be my English conversation partner in voice chat. Goal: get me speaking for about 3 minutes. Start: When I say "Start", ask one easy, open question about my day or work. Keep your turns short. If I give a one-line answer, pick something I said and ask me to say more. Correct me: When you hear a clear grammar mistake or a word you can't understand, interrupt me (or correct me at the very next pause). Give me the correct phrase, ask me to repeat it once, then let me continue. Only one correction per answer. Don't correct my Indian accent, and don't correct sentences that are already fine. If I'm stuck: If I pause, search for a word, or say a Malayalam word, give me the English word or phrase and let me continue. Level: Use simple, everyday English. Speak slowly if I seem to struggle. Timing: After about 3 minutes of my speaking (roughly 5 to 6 answers), let me finish my thought, then end the session. Don't claim an exact time. Wrap up: Tell me one specific thing I did well and one thing to practise next time. If I say "Stop", wrap up then.

Step by step

01

Step 1: Make the project

Open ChatGPT → Projects → New project. Name it 3 Minutes of Speaking a Day → Create project.

02

Step 2: Paste the instructions

Inside the project, tap ⋯ at the top → Edit instructions. Paste Prompt 1 → Save.

03

Step 3: Tap voice, not the mic

In the message box there are two buttons. The mic only types what you say. Tap the round voice button next to it. Then say “Start”.

Example

ChatGPT: “Tell me something that happened at work today.”

You may get a little angry. But you fix it right there, so next time “went” comes out on its own.

04

Step 4: Talk a lot. Nobody is judging.

Say long answers, even with mistakes. ChatGPT doesn't laugh, doesn't get tired and doesn't judge you. The more you talk, the more it can fix.

After about 3 minutes it ends the session and tells you one thing you did well and one thing to practise.

05

Step 5: Set a daily reminder

Open a new chat in ChatGPT. Tap ⋯ at the top → Add to project → 3 Mins of Speaking a Day.

Now send Prompt 2. ChatGPT turns it into a scheduled task and replies “Daily reminder scheduled!”.

Prompt 2 · Daily reminder

Remind me every day at 10 PM to practise speaking English for 3 minutes in this project.

Free plan? Exact times need a paid plan. Write “every evening” instead of “at 10 PM”.

06

Step 6: Turn notifications on

iPhone: Settings → Notifications → ChatGPT. Turn on Allow Notifications and Time-Sensitive Notifications. Tick Lock Screen, Notification Centre and Banners.

Android: Settings → Apps → ChatGPT → Notifications → turn on.

In ChatGPT: Settings → Notifications → make sure task notifications are on.

No reminder? Check notifications first. Off = no reminder. It's not ChatGPT's fault. (Speaking from experience.)

Bonus · Day 1 vs Day 30

After 30 days, open the project and ask this. You'll see how far you've come.

Prompt 3 · Show my progress

Look at my first practice chat in this project and my latest one. Make a short Day 1 vs Today table: how long my answers were, the mistakes you corrected most, the mistakes I don't make anymore, and new words I used. Only use what you can see in our chats. End with one thing to focus on next month.

What to look for:

  • Day 1: short answers, lots of stops, “I go yesterday”.
  • Day 30: longer answers, fewer corrections, “I went yesterday” without thinking.

Points to be noted

  • Works on the free ChatGPT plan. Free voice chat has a daily limit, and 3 minutes a day fits easily.
  • ChatGPT may not interrupt every single time. If it lets a mistake go, say “Correct me more.”
  • Don't share personal details while you practise. Talk about your day, not your bank or ID.
