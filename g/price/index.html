First: what's a scheduled task?

It's different from a normal chat. A normal chat answers you once. A scheduled task runs by itself every day and sends you a notification, even when the app is closed.

  • Free plan: up to 3 active tasks. They run once a day, in a time window like "morning" (not an exact time).
  • Plus: up to 5 active tasks, at exact times.
  • All your tasks live under Scheduled in ChatGPT's side menu. Pause or delete them there.

Step 1: Check the real price on BuyHatke

Sale prices are often raised first and then "cut". Check the real price before anything else.

  • On a browser: type buyhatke.com/ in front of the product link and open it.
  • On Android: tap Share on the product page and pick the Buyhatke app.
  • On iPhone: copy the link and paste it in the Buyhatke app's search bar.

You'll see a 3-month price chart with the lowest and average price. Use the lowest price as your target price.

Prompt 1 · Daily price alert (scheduled task)

Create a scheduled task that runs every morning. Task: Watch the price of the product below and tell me the right moment to buy it. Product: [PASTE YOUR PRODUCT LINK] Exact model: [model name, size, colour, storage] My target price: ₹[from BuyHatke, e.g. its lowest price] My cards: [e.g. HDFC credit card, SBI debit card] Each time it runs: 1. Open my product link and write down today's price exactly as shown on the page. 2. Search for the SAME model on Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital and the brand's own store. Skip any listing that is a different size, colour or version. 3. Check the bank offers for my cards and work out the final price I would actually pay. 4. Compare with my target price. Message me ONLY when the final price is at or below my target price. The message, short: Price drop: [product] ₹[price] on [store] → ₹[final] with [offer] Target: ₹[target] Link: [direct link] If the price is above my target, reply with one line only: "No drop yet: ₹[today's lowest] on [store]." Never guess or make up a price. If you can't open a store's page, say "couldn't check [store]".

02

Step 2: Create the task

Open the ChatGPT app on your phone, fill in the brackets in Prompt 1 and send it. ChatGPT replies that the task is set. Check it shows up under Scheduled in the side menu.

Make it from the phone app, so the alerts reach your phone.

03

Step 3: Turn notifications on

In ChatGPT go to Settings → Notifications and turn on Push (and Email if you like) for tasks. Also allow notifications for the ChatGPT app in your phone settings. No notifications, no alert.

04

Step 4: Buy, then turn it off

When the alert comes, open the link and check the price yourself. Buy with the card offer it mentioned. Then go to Scheduled and delete the task, so you have a free slot for the next product.

Good to know

  • One product per task. On the free plan you can watch 3 products at a time.
  • Card and bank offers depend on your card, so your final price can be different from mine.
  • ChatGPT can misread a price. Always check on the site before you pay.
  • BuyHatke can also send its own price alert. Use it as a backup.
