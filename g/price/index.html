Prompt · Price-drop alert

Create a scheduled task that runs every day at 9 AM. Task: Watch the price of this product for me and tell me the right moment to buy it. Each time it runs: 1. Open the product link below and note today's price, the listed price and any discount shown. 2. Search for the same model (same size, colour and storage) on Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital and the brand's own store. 3. Find the bank card, wallet and exchange offers I can apply, and work out the final price I would actually pay. 4. Check the price history (for example on BuyHatke or PriceHistory) and find the usual price and the lowest price so far. Message me ONLY if one of these is true: - the final price is the lowest it has been in the last 3 months, or - it is at least 15% below the usual price, or - it goes below my target price: ₹[YOUR TARGET PRICE, or leave blank]. When you message me, keep it short: - 🔔 Price drop: [product name] - Today: ₹… on [site] (usual price: ₹…, lowest so far: ₹…) - Offers to apply: [card / wallet / coupon] → final price ₹… - Verdict: Buy now / Wait - Link: [direct link] If there is no real drop, reply with one line only: "No drop today." Never make up a price. If you can't check a site, say so. Product link: [PASTE YOUR LINK HERE]

01

Step 1: Send the prompt

Copy the prompt, paste your product link at the end and send it in ChatGPT. It creates a daily scheduled task. This works on the free plan too.

02

Step 2: Turn notifications on

Allow notifications for the ChatGPT app, or you won't see the alert. You can see your task under Scheduled in ChatGPT's menu.

03

Step 3: Buy, then turn it off

When ChatGPT says Buy now, check the price on the site and buy. Then open Scheduled and pause or delete the task.

Good to know

  • Track one product per task. Make a new task for each thing you want to buy.
  • Bank and card offers depend on your card, so your final price can be different from mine.
  • Always check the price on the site before you pay. ChatGPT can be wrong.
